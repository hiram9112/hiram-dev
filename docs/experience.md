# Experience

## Direction

“Beyond the interface”: an English-language technical casebook that opens one real product to reveal the engineering within it. BeneHom carries the narrative; related systems are connected layers, not separate portfolio projects.

## Visual system

- Porcelain `#f5f6f8` and white `#ffffff` are the base; ink `#192b3d` carries text.
- Cobalt `#244ee8` defines the central case study; mist `#dce5ff` and signal gold `#edce83` support the system illustration.
- Manrope provides geometric display typography; DM Sans provides readable body copy.
- Large, left-aligned type and open space establish hierarchy. The system illustration is the primary visual artifact; supporting content uses restrained divisions rather than a card grid.

## Narrative and interaction

The opening introduces Hiram and an assembled, conceptual view of BeneHom. The main case study opens that view into five connected layers: application, Numa, MCP, automation and delivery. Scrolling updates the highlighted layer; direct chapter links provide an alternative. The drawing is explicitly conceptual, not a product screenshot or live dashboard.

On desktop the illustration sits beside the narrative. On phones it becomes a compact sticky view above the narrative. Reduced motion removes animated transitions and smooth scrolling while preserving layer selection and all content.

Three expandable engineering decisions connect the evidence to its implications. A proportionate professional profile leads to GitHub, LinkedIn, a printable technical CV, and professional contact through LinkedIn. The CV uses only the curated evidence; no email address or additional career history is inferred.
