# Product

## Purpose

Hiram Dev is a personal technical profile and engineering casebook.

Its purpose is to support professional opportunities by giving people who may consider working with Hiram clear evidence of:

- what he can build;
- how he approaches engineering problems;
- the technical depth of his work;
- his experience with real systems and production;
- and what public work can be inspected to verify those capabilities.

The site complements GitHub, LinkedIn and the CV. It does not replace them.

## Audience

The site is intended for professionals evaluating whether they would be interested in working with Hiram.

This includes, among others:

- recruiters;
- engineering managers;
- CTOs;
- developers involved in hiring;
- agencies;
- consultancies;
- technical partners.

Regardless of how they arrive at the site, they should be able to form an informed opinion about Hiram's capabilities, technical depth, way of working and suitability for a team, collaboration or project.

## Positioning

The site should behave more like a technical profile or engineering casebook than a conventional portfolio.

Core principle:

> Capability before self-promotion.

Professional claims should be supported whenever possible by concrete evidence such as working systems, code, technical decisions, production experience or inspectable implementations.

The site should not rely on disconnected skill lists or technology names as evidence of capability.

## V1 scope

The first version should provide enough information and evidence to understand:

- Hiram's current technical profile and areas of work;
- the capabilities he can currently demonstrate;
- BeneHom as the main body of public technical work;
- relevant subsystems and engineering work within BeneHom, including Numa, MCP, automation, quality and production;
- selected technical problems or decisions when they provide useful evidence of depth;
- the technologies that can be credibly supported by that work;
- where to continue inspecting Hiram's work and professional experience through GitHub, LinkedIn and the CV;
- how to contact him professionally.

The site must work well even if BeneHom is initially the only major public project.

The information architecture, narrative structure and visual presentation used to communicate this content are separate design decisions and are not defined by this document.

## Out of scope

The V1 is not:

- a commercial services site;
- a lead-generation funnel;
- an agency website;
- a client portal;
- a blog platform;
- a CMS;
- an authentication system;
- a database-backed application.

Backend functionality should only be introduced when a real product requirement justifies it.

## Product principles

- Demonstrate rather than claim.
- Prefer technical depth over project quantity.
- Keep important information easy to discover.
- Do not duplicate GitHub, LinkedIn or the CV unnecessarily.
- Avoid artificial projects created only to fill space.
- Do not add features merely to demonstrate technologies.
- Preserve accessibility, performance and mobile usability.
- Visual experimentation must support the professional purpose of the site.

## Success criteria

After reviewing the site, a recruiter, engineering manager, CTO, developer, agency, consultancy or technical partner should be able to answer:

1. What kind of developer is Hiram?
2. What can he actually build?
3. What technical depth does he demonstrate?
4. Has he worked with real systems and production?
5. How does he approach engineering problems?
6. What technologies can he credibly defend?
7. Where can I inspect his code and professional experience?
8. Could he contribute to my team, collaboration or project?
